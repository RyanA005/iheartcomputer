      blank → blank+spinner → skeleton → skeleton+spinner → stream → progressive

      feedback combos (spinner+skeleton often helps)

  4 remove the cloud (convenience vs control; vercel/supabase overhead)

      vercel+supabase vs own+sqlite (execution gap = remote rest vs embedded db)
      cold spawn p50 ~13ms (~8x vs warm); vercel cold start is the same idea
      c rewrite did not beat warm node+sqlite on this laptop
